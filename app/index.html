How accurate is this

I've tested this on 32 syllabi. Below are the accuracy scores as of 13 August 2026.

WhatRate

This personal project is pattern matching at the root of it, so a syllabus with an odd layout may throw it off. Anything it cannot read will need you to add or correct manually, rather than it quietly dropping a reading or inventing one. If there is no schedule, the syllabus may not have one, or there is a bug.
