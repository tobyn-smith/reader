Syllabi are written in every possible layout, and the parser is a set of patterns rather than a model. It handles bulleted lists, ruled tables, labelled blocks, date-led headings and numbered meetings. Where a schedule is missing entirely it is usually because the PDF has none: it lives on the course site, or the file carries only a deadline grid.

A reading it cannot parse is shown as the exact text from your syllabus and marked for checking. It is never dropped silently and never invented. That is why the review step exists, and why it is worth the few minutes.
